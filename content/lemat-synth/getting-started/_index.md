- [Quickstart]({{< ref "/lemat-synth/getting-started/quickstart" >}}) — go from zero to your first extracted synthesis in under 10 minutes
- [Dataset Access]({{< ref "/lemat-synth/getting-started/dataset" >}}) — load the published LeMat-Synth dataset from HuggingFace, no extraction required
